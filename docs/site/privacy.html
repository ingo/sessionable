Privacy policy

Sessionable collects nothing about you.

What the app does with data

Sessionable has no accounts, no backend, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not collect, store on a server or share any personal data.

The only network traffic is a download of the public schedule of the conference you connect to, from sessionize.com. That request carries the conference id you entered, as any web browser would.

What stays on your device

Your bookmarks, notes, ratings, the people you marked as met and any photos or videos you attach to a session are stored on your device only. You add a photo or video only from a session's own page, with the camera or the system picker. Deleting a conference in the app deletes its notes and attachments, and so does deleting the app.

Notifications are local reminders scheduled on your device. No push service is used.

Permissions

Camera and microphone are used only when you take a photo or record a video for a session. Notifications are used only for the session reminders you switch on.

Not affiliated with Sessionize

Sessionable is an independent, open-source app. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Sessionize.
