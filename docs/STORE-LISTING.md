# Store listing text

Drafts of the App Store and Google Play text in each language the app speaks. Review the translations with a native
speaker before you submit, and decide the app name first (see the name and trademark note in `RELEASING.md`). Limits:
App Store name 30, subtitle 30, keywords 100 (comma-separated, no spaces after commas), promotional text 170;
Google Play title 30, short description 80; both 4000 for the full description.

The privacy answers are the same in every language: App Store "Data Not Collected"; Play data safety "no data collected
or shared". Keep the line that says the app is unofficial in every description.

## English (en-US)

- **App Store subtitle:** Any Sessionize conference
- **Play short description:** Any Sessionize conference, in your pocket
- **Keywords:** conference,schedule,sessionize,agenda,speakers,sessions,event,programme,bookmarks,notes
- **Description:**

  Sessionable is a conference app for any event that publishes its schedule on Sessionize. Paste the conference's
  Sessionize link and you have its sessions, speakers and rooms, with nothing to sign up for.

  - See what is on now and what is next, with the room and how long is left
  - Bookmark sessions and see your own timeline, with clashes marked
  - Search the schedule and filter by topic, level and format
  - Keep private notes on sessions and speakers, rate what you saw, and mark the people you met
  - Add a session or your whole schedule to your calendar, and share it
  - Attach photos and videos to a session, from the session's own page
  - Session reminders on your device

  No account, no ads, no tracking. Your bookmarks, notes and photos stay on your device. The app only downloads the
  public schedule from Sessionize.

  Sessionable is an independent, open-source app. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Sessionize.
